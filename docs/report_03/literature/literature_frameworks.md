### Turner et al. (2009) - Health Insurance Comparability
**Source:** PMC3034271
**Key concepts:**
- CPS-ASEC "any time during year" produces point-in-time-like estimates due to respondent interpretation
- State-level discrepancies up to 5 percentage points between ACS and CPS
**Relevant for taxonomy:** TC (temporal) case study, CC (construct) for Medicaid naming

### Davern et al. (2009) - ACS-CPS Comparability  
**Source:** PMC2677056
**Key concepts:**
- ACS "past 12 months" rolling vs CPS "calendar year" creates 0.7-0.8 correlation for same households
- Reference period mismatch largest source of discrepancy
**Relevant for taxonomy:** TC (temporal) quantified impact

### Federal Reserve (2025) - Homeownership Measurement
**Source:** FEDS 2025-010
**Key concepts:**
- SHED "respondent or spouse own" vs Census "you or someone in household"
- Creates 8-9 percentage point "neither own nor rent" inflation in SHED
**Relevant for taxonomy:** CC (construct) operationalization difference

### Census Bureau (2015) - ACS Burden Reduction Feasibility
**Source:** census.gov methodology document
**Key concepts:**
- "Option 4: Administrative Records Hybrid" recommended
- Matrix sampling design (Groups A/B/C)
- Estimated 20-30% burden reduction possible
**Relevant for taxonomy:** Feasibility assessment methodology

### National Academies (2018) - Economic Survey Harmonization (EXPANDED)
**Source:** NAP 25098 Chapter 6
**Key concepts:**
- Content harmonization teams (8-12 members)
- Centralized question repository
- Feasibility studies before implementation
- Mixed data sources approach
**Relevant for taxonomy:** Implementation framework, governance model
