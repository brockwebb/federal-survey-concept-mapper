## Dual Value Proposition

### Primary Goal (Why This Matters)
**Respondent burden reduction**
- Federal surveys ask overlapping questions
- Declining response rates increase costs
- Consolidation reduces redundant questions for the public

### Secondary Value (How We Get There Faster)
**Expert efficiency through AI assistance**
- Expert time is scarce resource
- Traditional analysis takes weeks/months
- AI-assisted approach: < 1 day processing
- Experts focus on highest-value decisions

**Key Connection:** Expert efficiency → faster burden reduction
